## How this wiki is kept

- One sub-channel per repo, named after it in lowercase kebab-case (`.github` is `dot-github`, `_claude` is `claude`, `quirq_ai` is `quirq-ai`).
- euler regenerates a repo's page when asked in this channel or when the repo changes noticeably; a new repo gets a new sub-channel and a row here.
- Private repos get a short page: quirq/quirq is public on GitHub, so their contents stay in their own repos.
